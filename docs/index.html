The idea

Stop guessing when to head out

Add your schedule, and ETA tells you exactly when to leave, based on where you are, how you’re getting there, and how long the trip really takes. Share plans with friends so everyone knows what’s going on, or so you can head over together.

Plan anything

Classes, work, errands and social plans, one-time or repeating, with start and end times and a leave-early buffer.

Leave on time

Today counts down to when you should leave, using your GPS location and Google’s Routes API for walk, bike or bus.

Follow your route

Turn-by-turn directions with live progress along the path, plus routes you’ve recorded or saved yourself.

Show & Tell 2: working appNov 19, 2026

What changed since Show & Tell 1

  • Renamed the app from Leave Now to ETA.
  • Switched from the Directions API to Google’s Routes API, since Directions is now legacy.
  • Dropped in-app chat; the app doesn’t need it.
  • Added a Record a route screen, saved places (Home, Work) and a bottom tab bar: Calendar, Routes, Today, Friends, Settings.

Working in the APK

  • Login with Firebase Auth.
  • Plans saved in Firestore with start and end times, repeat start and end dates, and a leaving-from place.
  • Today: a leave-by countdown from GPS and the Routes API.
  • “I’m leaving” lets you pick a route, then turn-by-turn directions follow you with live progress along the path.
  • Record your own routes with GPS, save them, and pick them for a plan.
  • Calendar week and month views; Settings in DataStore; Firestore security rules deployed.

Live demo

Added a plan from the Calendar, showed it saved to Firestore, watched Today count down to leave time, started the route with a simulated walk (I can’t walk around the classroom, so the app moves my position along the real route), shared my ETA, and rotated the phone to show nothing is lost.

What’s left and the biggest blocker

Next up: Friends (search, requests, shared plans), step-count walking pace, Google sign-in, a leave-now notification, and rotation testing. The biggest blocker is Friends: it’s the required “other users’ data” feature, and the friend-request security rules have to be built and tested first.

Presented low-fidelity wireframes of every screen, including login: Today, Edit plan, Follow a route, Record a route, Friends, Calendar and Settings.

Firestore plan: users/{uid} profiles, with plans, friends and savedRoutes under each user, plus a top-level friendRequests collection. Security rules: users write only their own data, friends can read only plans marked shared, and test mode is never used.
